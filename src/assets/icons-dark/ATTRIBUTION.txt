They are the dark-mode variants, generated by scripts/build-dark-icons.ps1 from
the pack's *shadowless* originals (/assets/fugue-icons-3.5.6/icons-shadowless,
which is gitignored). The script flips the lightness of desaturated pixels only,
so the black/grey linework reads on a dark background while coloured artwork is
left untouched. Modification of the original work is permitted under CC-BY 3.0
and is noted here as required.

Do not hand-edit these files: re-run the script instead.

  powershell -File scripts/build-dark-icons.ps1

The light-mode set in ../icons is the unmodified pack art and stays authoritative
for which icons exist -- the script generates one dark icon per light icon.
